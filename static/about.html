CLEAR PURPOSE · RESPONSIBLE ACCESS

An organized space for every step.

VerifyFlow supports documented, role-based reviews while giving students a secure place to follow academic requests and manage public portfolios. Student contact details and private submissions remain access-controlled.

The portal complements — it does not replace — the college's primary website and formal academic processes.

HOW THE WORKFLOW FUNCTIONS

From submission to review.

Simple, accountable steps with dedicated access for each participant.

01 · Faculty

Upload academic documents for departmental rule checks and assisted review.

02 · HOD

Set requirements, review reports, assign tasks and request corrections.

03 · Principal & IQAC

Access the approved institutional records relevant to their responsibilities.
