Help

Simple answers before you sign in

How do I get a student account?

An institution administrator can enroll student accounts for active departments. Use the temporary credentials given to you privately and change your password at first sign-in.

Who can see an issue I raise?

Your private request is available only to you and your department HOD. It is not part of the student directory or public search results.

Will my personal contact details appear online?

No. Students can choose to publish their portfolio, but the public directory does not display mobile numbers or email addresses. Review certificate links before sharing them.
