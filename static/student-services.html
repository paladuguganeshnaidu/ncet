How it works

Three simple steps

Your private academic requests stay with your authorized reviewers.

1

Sign in using your USN

Authorized faculty register students. Your USN starts with 1NC. Change your temporary password at your first sign-in.

2

Describe the issue

Choose an area such as academics, examinations, facilities or a general request. Be specific without posting unnecessary private information.

3

Follow your case

Track submissions, read HOD replies, and see when a request is reviewed or resolved.

Examples of requests

  • Academic timetable and class concerns
  • Examination or assessment questions
  • Study resources and academic documents
  • Facilities or student-support requests

Private requests stay private

Only your authenticated student account and authorized department HODs can read case details and responses. Faculty can enroll students but cannot browse private complaints.

This service is for routine academic issues. For immediate safety emergencies, contact campus security or local emergency services directly.
