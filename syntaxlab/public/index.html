[ 05 / LEARN THE LANGUAGE ]THEORY OF COMPUTATION

BEHIND
THE RULES.

01REGULAR EXPRESSIONS+

Regular expressions describe sets of strings through character classes, concatenation, alternation, and repetition. In this project they specify the allowed email and URL syntax.

EMAIL := LOCAL "@" DOMAIN
02DETERMINISTIC FINITE AUTOMATA+

A DFA is a five-tuple M = (Q, Σ, δ, q₀, F). One character is consumed per transition; the machine accepts only when the input ends in an accepting state.

δ : Q × Σ → Q
03EMAIL GRAMMAR+

Our project supports unquoted ASCII dot-separated local atoms and letter/digit/hyphen domain labels. A dot and a final alphabetic suffix of at least two letters are required. This is intentionally narrower than the complete email RFC.

LOCAL @ LABEL (. LABEL)+
04URL GRAMMAR+

We accept HTTP(S) URLs with a domain or localhost, an optional numeric port, and path, query, or fragment. The parser rejects malformed percent escapes and control characters. Userinfo, IPv6, and IDN are out of scope for v1.

SCHEME :// HOST [PORT] [PATH] [QUERY] [FRAGMENT]
05ACCEPTANCE & LIMITATIONS+

Syntax validation alone cannot confirm mailbox ownership, mail deliverability, site reachability, or URL safety. Length and numeric-port checks supplement the simplified automata.

ACCEPT ≠ EXISTS ≠ SAFE
